Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-22

Our Privacy Promise

Paint Online functions as a zero-cost artistic platform. Enrollment remains optional, and we prioritize safeguarding information through restricted gathering practices.

Information We Handle

  • Local data: game activity is stored temporarily in your browser’s localStorage to track recent sessions. This data is never sent to our servers and is not personally identifiable.
  • Contact messages: if you choose to contact us via our form, we receive the information you submit to respond to your inquiry.

Tracking and Marketing

Cookie technology remains absent. The whole system avoids commercial promotions and functions independently on your device to guarantee protection.

Your Preferences

  • Clearing your browser's local storage erases any retained sessions whenever desired.
  • Activating anonymous browsing prevents preservation of operation information.

Get in Contact

Questions or concerns? Navigate to our homepage's contact portal for assistance.
